# Momentum Strategy

A cross-sectional momentum strategy based on Jegadeesh and Titman (1993).

## Main concepts:

- Cross-sectional stock ranking
- Momentum factor construction
- Long-short portfolio formation
- Backtesting
- Risk-adjusted performance analysis

## Methodology:

- Data sample : S&P 500 constituents (2020–2025)
- Controlled survivorship bias by including historical index additions and removals
- Formation period: 6 months
- Skip period: 1 month
- Holding period: 1 month
- Long top 10% winners
- Short bottom 10% losers
